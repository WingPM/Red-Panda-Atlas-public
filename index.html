Did You Know?

Red pandas use their long, bushy tails as blankets in winter and as balance aids while climbing trees. Despite their name, they are not bears!

Species Spotlight

More than a small panda

Red pandas are the only living members of the family Ailuridae, native to the temperate forests of the Himalayas and southwestern China.

  • Scientific Name Ailurus fulgens
  • Lifespan 8-10 years in the wild; up to 15 in human care
  • Diet Bamboo leaves, berries, blossoms, bird eggs
  • Status Endangered on the IUCN Red List
